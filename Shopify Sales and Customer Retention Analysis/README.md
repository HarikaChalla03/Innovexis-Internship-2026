# Shopify Sales & Customer Retention Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes Shopify sales and customer behavior to understand revenue performance, purchasing frequency, customer segmentation, geographic customer value, and customer lifetime value (CLV).

The objective is to identify customer retention opportunities and provide data-driven recommendations for improving repeat purchases and customer value.

##  Business Problem

The business needs to answer:

* How is overall sales performance?
* How frequently are customers purchasing?
* Which customers or locations generate higher value?
* Where are potential customer-retention opportunities?
* How can customer lifetime value be improved?

##  Key KPIs

| KPI                |   Value |
| ------------------ | ------: |
| Revenue            | 132.30K |
| Customers          |     212 |
| Orders             |     217 |
| Purchase Frequency |    1.02 |
| Washington CLV     |  670.35 |
| Staten Island CLV  |  546.84 |

##  Key Findings

1. The analysis generated a total revenue of **132.30K** from **212 customers** and **217 orders**.
2. Purchase frequency was **1.02**, indicating relatively limited repeat purchasing in the analyzed dataset.
3. Customer lifetime value varied across locations.
4. Washington recorded a CLV of **670.35**, while Staten Island recorded **546.84**.
5. Customer segmentation provides a way to identify higher-value customers and potential retention opportunities.

##  Business Insights

* Increasing repeat purchases is an important opportunity for improving customer value.
* Customers should not be treated as one homogeneous group because purchasing behavior and lifetime value differ.
* Geographic CLV analysis can help identify markets that warrant deeper investigation.
* Retention metrics should be monitored alongside revenue and customer acquisition metrics.

##  Business Investigation

The analysis can be extended by investigating:

* Why is purchase frequency relatively low?
* Which customers make repeat purchases?
* Which customer segments generate the highest revenue?
* Is high CLV driven by higher order value, more purchases, or both?
* Which locations have high customer value?
* Which customers may be at risk of becoming inactive?

##  Business Recommendations

1. Develop second-purchase campaigns to encourage customers to return.
2. Use RFM segmentation to identify high-value and inactive customers.
3. Introduce personalized offers, product recommendations, or loyalty initiatives.
4. Analyze high-CLV locations for targeted customer strategies.
5. Monitor repeat purchase rate, purchase frequency, AOV, retention rate, and CLV.

#  Coffee Shop Sales & Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes coffee-shop transaction data using **Power BI, DAX, Power Query, and Excel** to understand sales performance, product contribution, store performance, and time-based purchasing patterns.

The objective was to transform transaction-level data into an interactive business dashboard that helps management monitor performance, identify growth opportunities, and support data-driven operational decisions.

---

##  Business Problem

The coffee-shop business needs a consolidated view of its sales performance to answer questions such as:

* How are sales changing over time?
* Which products contribute the most revenue?
* Which stores perform better?
* What are the peak transaction hours?
* How does Average Order Value change?
* Which products and locations require further investigation?
* What actions can improve sales and operational planning?

##  Key Business Findings

* Total sales from January–June 2023 were approximately **₹698.81K**.
* **June** recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately **₹166.49K**.
* February experienced a **6.77% decline** compared with January.
* Sales increased strongly from March through June.
* **Coffee** generated approximately **₹269.95K** in sales.
* **Tea** generated approximately **₹196.41K**.
* **Hell's Kitchen** generated approximately **₹234.51K**.
* Sales contribution was relatively balanced across the three stores.
* Average Order Value remained approximately **₹4.65–₹4.72**.
* Transactions were concentrated within the observed operating period of approximately **06:00–20:00**.

---

##  Business Insights

### Sales Growth

Sales strengthened considerably after February, with particularly strong month-over-month increases in March and May.

### Product Performance

Coffee and tea were important revenue contributors, making product availability and inventory planning important areas for management attention.

### Store Performance

The three locations showed relatively balanced sales contribution, suggesting that performance should be evaluated beyond revenue alone using transactions, AOV, product mix, and operating costs.

### Customer Spending

The relatively stable AOV suggests that transaction volume and customer traffic are important areas to investigate when explaining overall sales growth.

### Time-Based Demand

Transaction-hour analysis can help identify periods of higher demand and support operational planning.

##  Business Recommendations

### 1. Optimize inventory

Maintain sufficient inventory for high-performing products, particularly during high-demand periods.

### 2. Investigate sales growth drivers

Analyze the factors behind the strong March–June growth and identify repeatable drivers.

### 3. Align staffing with demand

Use transaction-hour patterns to better align staffing with customer demand.

### 4. Increase Average Order Value

Test product bundles such as beverage + snack combinations and monitor the effect on AOV and transaction volume.

### 5. Develop store-specific strategies

Use store-level product and time patterns to customize inventory and promotional decisions.

### 6. Establish recurring KPI monitoring

Track:

**Sales → Transactions → AOV → Store → Product → Hour**

on a regular basis.
